BEE Laboratory · Sogang University

Disruptive battery technologies by chemistry, materials, engineering.

Advanced battery materials and systems bring a strong complexity that demands an insightful understanding of electrochemical phenomena. Our group establishes principles for material design and rigorously evaluates these innovations to bring them toward practical standards.
